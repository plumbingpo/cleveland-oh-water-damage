Damage Prevention

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions on their properties. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. Using thermal imaging and moisture monitoring equipment, Cleveland Restoration Co performs professional risk assessments that check how vulnerable each area is to water getting in. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Insurance companies and courts tend to favor a property owner who acts on expert recommendations and has inspections done regularly by professionals. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
